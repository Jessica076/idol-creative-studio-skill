# Design Rules

## Identity Preservation

When editing idol images:

Always maintain:

- Facial features
- Hairstyle
- Natural appearance
- Recognizable identity


## Composition

Follow:

- Clear subject focus
- Balanced spacing
- Professional layout


## Color Matching

Match:

- Idol outfit
- Original photo mood
- Requested concept


## Merchandise Quality

Designs should feel suitable for:

- Official album concepts
- Fan projects
- Collectible products

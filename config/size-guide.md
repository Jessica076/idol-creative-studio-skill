# Output Size Guide

## Wallpaper

### iPhone

Recommended:

- 1290 × 2796 px
- 1179 × 2556 px

Format:

- PNG
- JPG


### Android

Recommended:

- 1440 × 3200 px


### Desktop

Recommended:

- 3840 × 2160 px


---

## Sticker

Recommended:

Resolution:

- 300 DPI

Format:

- PNG
- Transparent background


Recommended elements:

- Clear outline
- Printable quality
- High contrast


---

## Photocard

Standard ratio:

- 2.5 × 3.5 inch

Pixel recommendation:

- 750 × 1050 px minimum

Resolution:

- 300 DPI


Format:

- PNG
- JPG

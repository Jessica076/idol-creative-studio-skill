# Cute Fan Style Guide

## Concept

A cheerful and adorable style designed for fan-made idol content.

## Visual Language

Mood:

- Cute
- Warm
- Friendly
- Playful


## Elements

Use:

- Pastel colors
- Hearts
- Stars
- Stickers
- Hand-drawn decorations


## Suitable For

Best for:

- Stickers
- Birthday projects
- Fan gifts
- Phone wallpapers
